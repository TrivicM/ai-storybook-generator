The Little Moon Dragon lived above the silver clouds.

Every evening he painted tiny stars across the night sky.

He carried a glowing lantern that made sleepy children dream sweet dreams.

One windy night, the lantern suddenly disappeared.

The Little Moon Dragon searched through forests, rivers, and mountains looking for its magical light.

Along the way he met a brave rabbit, a singing owl, and a giant turtle covered in moss.

Together they followed sparkling footprints through the dark forest.

At the top of the tallest hill, they discovered the lantern resting beside a sleeping fox.

The fox had protected the lantern from the storm all night long.

The Little Moon Dragon thanked his new friends and flew back into the sky.

That evening the stars shined brighter than ever before.

And from that day on, the Little Moon Dragon was never alone again.
