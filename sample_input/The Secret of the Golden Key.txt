Once upon a time, in the middle of the Whispering Woods, three unlikely best friends lived in a cozy hollow tree: Barnaby the Rabbit, who loved to bounce; Roxy the Fox, who loved to explore; and Toby the Turtle, who loved to take his sweet, sweet time.

One sunny afternoon, Roxy pointed her sharp nose toward the Big Willow Tree. "Look! A shiny golden key is hanging from the branch!"

"Let’s get it!" squeaked Barnaby. With a boing-boing-boing, he zipped through the tall grass, leaving everyone behind. But when he reached the tree, the key was much too high. He jumped and he bounced, but he just couldn't reach it.

Roxy trotted up, her bushy tail wagging. "Move over, Barnaby! Let an expert climber try." Roxy scrambled up the rough bark of the tree. She climbed high, but the branch with the key was too thin and wiggly for her paws. "Oh no," she whimpered, stuck on a branch. "I'm stuck, and I still can't reach it!"

Just then, plod, plod, plod, Toby the Turtle finally arrived. He looked at Barnaby, who was tired from bouncing. He looked up at Roxy, who was scared to come down. Toby smiled. "Don't worry, friends. We just need to work together!"

Toby crawled right underneath Roxy’s branch. "Roxy, slide down onto my strong, hard shell!"

Whoosh! Roxy slid down like it was a playground slide. Thump! She landed safely on Toby's back.

"Now," Toby said, "Barnaby, hop onto Roxy’s shoulders!"

Barnaby did a gentle mini-hop onto Roxy’s back. Roxy stood up on her tippy-toes, balancing carefully on Toby. Barnaby stretched his long rabbit ears and his fluffy paws as high as he could. Snatch! He grabbed the golden key!

"We did it!" they all cheered, tumbling into a happy pile of soft green moss.

They used the golden key to open a tiny wooden chest hidden in the roots of the willow tree. Inside, they found the best treasure of all: three delicious, sweet strawberries—one for the rabbit, one for the fox, and one for the turtle.

The Moral of the Woods: Big or small, fast or slow, teamwork makes the adventure go!
